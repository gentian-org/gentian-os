# Gentian OS v0.5

**Milestone:** a control plane of eight programs, a cluster laid out by tier,
and a platform that is itself a tenant made of components.
**Compares against:** [v0.4.2](https://github.com/gentian-org/gentian-os/releases/tag/v0.4.2)
(2026-10-09) — 1,768 commits.
**Cut with:** the runbook in [deployment.md §5.4](../guides/deployment.md).

v0.4 made an installed app changeable, exportable and restorable, and turned
the installer into a driver. v0.5 rebuilds what runs those apps. One operator
with one all-powerful credential became eight programs with one standing
credential each; namespaces named after products became a layout named after
functions; the portal and the in-cluster App Store became components a catalog
places into the platform's own tenant; and `AppProfile` became
`ComponentProfile`, installed from digest-pinned bundles with a version floor,
a maintenance window and a guarded move.

This is a new cluster layout, not a patch over the old one. A v0.4 cluster is
not carried forward in place; its tenants are (§15).

---

## 1. One control plane, eight programs

The operator used to do everything with one credential. v0.5 splits that
authority along what each part may write
([control-plane.md](../concepts/control-plane.md), AD-2):

| Program | Writes | Holds |
|---|---|---|
| director | the deployments repository, as signed commits | the git key |
| operator | the cluster | the cluster credential |
| custodian | secrets, write-only | the vault token |
| registrar | people and groups in Keycloak, its own database | the realm credential |
| usher | nothing: answers tiles, states and approvals | a read-only key |
| bouncer | nothing: decides at the Gateway | the authorization key |
| arbiter | the apps' authorization store | the app OpenFGA key |
| concierge | nothing: the page on the bare domain | no credential |

`kubectl gentian` is a client of the director: every change an administrator
makes is a signed commit Argo CD applies, and Argo CD refuses an unsigned head
(`AppProject.spec.sourceIntegrity`). The operator's listener admits callers by
ServiceAccount. Keycloak and OpenFGA are Argo CD Applications of the identity
ApplicationSet with vendored subcharts; the `Suze` kind that composed them is
gone. The Crossplane definitions arrive through Argo CD too
(`gentian-definitions-<stage>`), so B-06 is a first-install step and an
operator or director that finds its definitions outdated reports
`DefinitionsOutdated` and refuses writes.

## 2. The cluster laid out by tier

Namespaces are named for a function and labeled with a tier, and policy reads
the labels ([namespaces.md](../reference/namespaces.md), AD-7 to AD-11):

- **`kernel-<function>`**: `gitops`, `provisioning`, `secrets`, `seal`, `data`,
  `authentication`, `authorization`, `control`, `edge`, `admission`,
  `observability`.
- **`system-<function>`**, and `system-<function>-dmz` where something is
  published: `postgresql`, `mariadb`, `cache`, `s3`, `authorization`, `turn`,
  `mail`, `llm`. PostgreSQL and the object store always run; the rest is
  composed when the first profile declares a need for it and stays until a
  person purges it (`spec.systemServices.always` forces one).
- **`tenant-<t>`** and `tenant-<t>-dmz`.

`platform-kernel`, `crossplane-system`, `cert-manager`,
`envoy-gateway-system`, `argocd`, `openbao`, `gentian-infra-<env>` and
`gentian-dev` no longer exist. `make lint-namespace-layout` and
`lint-retired-namespaces` keep the repository to the layout. Kernel ingress is
default-deny from generated NetworkPolicies (`KERNEL_NETWORK_POLICIES`), so a
missing allowance shows as a timeout rather than a refusal
([install.md §9](../reference/install.md)).

Headlamp gives a cluster administrator a view of the cluster under their own
identity, through kube-oidc-proxy, in `kernel-observability`.

## 3. The platform is a tenant

The portal is gone as a thing of its own. The platform has a tenant,
`tenant-platform`, which adopts the kernel realm; its desktop, administration
console and App Store are components placed from a catalog like any other
app's, and the concierge publishes the page on the cluster's bare domain from
that tenant's DMZ (AD-10).

- The platform administrator signs in at `https://platform.<domain>/`; the
  console is `admin.platform.<domain>`, identity `id.<domain>`.
- A tenant is reached at `desktop.<tenant>.<domain>`, `admin.<tenant>.<domain>`
  and `store.<tenant>.<domain>`. Single tenancy is the platform tenant plus
  one `user` tenant (E-04) at `desktop.<domain>`.
- The desktop, the administration console and the concierge come from
  gentian-ui as charts published twice: a moving prerelease per branch and an
  immutable one per commit. The installer resolves the first into the second
  and pins it (`PORTAL_IMAGE_TAG`, §15).
- The App Store app ships from the store's catalog (`app-store-pe`), not from
  the operator chart. Step 0 places the store's extra services unless
  `--no-extra-services` is given, which is recorded on the claim
  (`spec.catalogue.extraServices: false`).
- A website may take the main address, acknowledged by an approver
  (`mainAddress.tenant`).

## 4. Components, builds and catalogs

`AppProfile` and `AppCatalogue` are replaced
([app-catalog.md](../concepts/app-catalog.md), AD-3 to AD-6, AD-14):

- **`ComponentProfile`** is the cluster-scoped catalog entry, classes `app`
  and `adapter` (`service` and `shared-app` are schema only). It declares
  `package` (chart, composition, API or addon, with images, `extraValues`
  and `valueMapping`), `requires` (`platform` floor, `services`,
  `privileges`), `provides`, `integrations`, `expose[]` with a mandatory
  `authMode` and `surface`, `launch`, `secrets` (generated, supplied,
  derived), `hooks`, `extensions`, `placement`, `classes`, `version` and
  `sessionMaxAge`.
- **`Component`** is the namespaced instance; **`ProfileBuild`** is one per
  installed build digest, committed under `clusters/<c>/catalogue/builds/`.
- **A catalog is https or an OCI artifact**, read with the owner's repository
  credential; the directory source in the deployments repository is gone.
  Sources are declared per cluster and per tenant and managed with
  `kubectl gentian catalogues add|remove|rename|residue`
  ([custom-catalogs.md](../guides/custom-catalogs.md)). The store's own
  catalogs are added from its `GET /v1/meta`.
- **The operator renders every chart.** The app Composition and the second
  delivery path are gone; a composed app is handed over in place.
- **Placement.** Every component of a tenant is a `spec.apps[]` entry.
  Entries the platform places carry `placed: true`, are written only by the
  director's placement worker, and cannot be installed or removed by hand
  (409). Open WebUI is placed with the `llm` capability.
- **Privileges are approved, by kind.** The install asks for them; egress goes
  to a perimeter approver, pod security and cluster roles to a security
  officer, API extensions and writing roles to break-glass. `kubectl gentian
  approvals list|granted|cluster-roles` shows and grants them; the deployment's
  `_base.yaml` allowlist is seeded by the director.
- **Add-ons** are switched on inside their base and never installed beside it
  (422 if tried), granted on their own (`addonGrants`), named by what the app
  calls them, and move with their app when pinned.
- **Editions `ce · me · ie · pe · ee`:** community, maintained community code,
  the operator's own, proprietary free, paid. A cluster's own listing shows
  the first three; the last two are the store's.
- **Kernel URL keys and kernel-egress labels** are honored only at
  `trustTier: platform`, which a tenant's catalog cannot claim.
- The store contract moved from API 1.0 to 1.15
  ([store-api](../reference/store-api/store-api.md)): reviews, tenant access,
  support offers, maturity, seats, editions, registration, terms, free
  acquisition and partner assertions in place of a store sign-in.

## 5. Versioning and updates

A platform knows its version and a build declares its floor
([versioning.md](../concepts/versioning.md)):

- The chart carries `0.5.0` on a release and `0.(Y+1).0-dev` between releases;
  `make gen-platform-version` writes the version line the operator and the
  director compare against, and the git ref the Cluster composition reads the
  infra charts at ([deployment.md §5.5](../guides/deployment.md)).
  `Cluster.status.platform` reports line, build and the newest release.
- A profile declares `spec.requires.platform: ">=0.5"`; CI holds both
  published catalogs to it.
- **Update modes** `latest`, `manual` and `tenant` on `spec.catalogue.updates`
  and per app on `Tenant.spec.apps[].updateMode`, with a maintenance `window`,
  and an update report (`apps outdated`, `move-outdated`, `update-mode`).
- **A move is one guarded operation:** refuse a floor or digest the platform
  does not know, take a fresh export, materialize, verify after the rollout,
  roll back on failure, and report in `status.rollout`.
- **Image tags.** A branch install pins `<branch>-<short-sha>` of the checkout
  and every gentian-os Application follows that commit; a release tag runs its
  version; a moving tag is warned about and refused on `prod`.
- **Every upstream chart the installer brings is pinned in `versions.yaml`:**
  OpenBao, Reloader, CNPG, Kyverno, external-dns, Headlamp and Postfix beside
  the Crossplane packages. The bootstrap chart and the mail ApplicationSet
  read the pins, and `lint-version-pins` refuses a literal version or
  repository in the chart templates, the workflows and the scripts.

## 6. Identity, roles and authorization

- **Cluster roles live in git** (AD-16). `spec.platformAccess.roleHolders[]`
  names a subject per role — `securityOfficer`, `auditor`, `serviceAdmin`,
  `sharedAppsAdmin`, `perimeterApprover`, `tenantAuditor`, `breakGlass` with a
  required expiry — with a reason; `kubectl gentian platform-roles
  holders|candidates|members|review` reads and reviews them
  (`platformAccess.accessReview`). The cluster administrator approves every
  tenant's perimeter by default; `Tenant.spec.platformAccess.adminsEnter` lets
  platform administrators into a tenant.
- **Authorization model v1**, the kernel store pinned by id in the
  `authorization-store` ConfigMap; readers wait for the model they were built
  with.
- **An authorization service for apps**
  ([app-authorization.md](../concepts/app-authorization.md)): a profile
  declares `requires.services.authorization` with a model, mirrored groups and
  tuples; a second OpenFGA runs in `system-authorization` behind the arbiter,
  one store per tenant and component, mirrored from Keycloak events and
  carried in the export bundle (format 5).
- **Partners** (AD-17, [partners.md](../concepts/partners.md),
  [identity-brokering.md](../guides/identity-brokering.md)):
  `Tenant.spec.partners`, `spec.partnerCommissioners` and
  `Cluster.spec.partners`; a profile asks for `requires.services.partners`
  and receives the `partner-<name>` scope with `gentian_roles`. The store's
  vendor is written as every tenant's partner. This replaces
  `spec.store.signIn`, `brokeredFrom`, `brokeredTo` and
  `organizationCommissioners`.
- **Sign-up tenants** (`signUp.enabled`, `signUp.home`), `spec.country`,
  `locales`, `TenantDomain`, and `spec.organization` (legal name, currency,
  time zone, fiscal year, address, registers), delivered to apps through
  `valueMapping.platform.organizationKey`.
- **Vouching:** `requires.services.vouching.director` lets a platform
  component act with a cluster administrator's token on named routes.
- **Hardening:** MFA is required for groups marked `gentianRequiresMFA` in
  every realm; the password policy is twelve characters with the last five
  kept; the operator, its Jobs and provider-keycloak sign in as the
  `gentian-operator` service account; one OpenFGA preshared key per kernel
  service; kernel PostgreSQL with `sslmode=require`; the Gateway reaches Argo
  CD over TLS; kernel NetworkPolicies on by default.
- The vault after the handover is maintained by a `vault-migrator` Job under
  its own role; an upgrade needs no root token.

## 7. The installer and the handover

- **Step 0** replaces `--prepare-deployment`. It asks every setting with its
  default, writes the Cluster claim, the stage profiles and the platform
  tenant, commits them signed with the break-glass and director keys in
  `~/.gentian/gnupg` and pushes. The deployments token needs write access.
  Existing definitions are never asked again.
- **Steps are renumbered** A-01 to E-05
  ([install-steps.md](../reference/install-steps.md)); E-04 creates the user
  tenant, E-05 waits for the operator's objects.
- **The handover is an activation link.** `admin@<kernel-domain>` has no
  password; a single-use link is mailed to `CLUSTER_ADMIN_RECOVERY_EMAIL` or
  shown once, MFA is enrolled unless `CLUSTER_ADMIN_REQUIRE_MFA` is off, and
  the installer waits until the Credentials tab of the console has been opened
  (`GENTIAN_HANDOVER_WAIT_SECS`). `--activate-admin` and
  `kubectl gentian tenants activate-admin` reissue a link.
- **Unattended installs:** `GENTIAN_NONINTERACTIVE=1` with
  `GENTIAN_KIT_RECIPIENT` (an age public key), `GENTIAN_DEPLOYMENTS_REPO` and
  `KERNEL_DOMAIN`; credentials and claim answers come from the environment
  ([install.md](../reference/install.md)).
- **Pre-flight** requires Kubernetes 1.33, a CNI that enforces NetworkPolicy,
  `git` and `gpg` on the path, the recorded break-glass key (`--recover
  <kit>`, `--rotate-break-glass-key`), and reports the secrets-at-rest
  verdict (`GENTIAN_REQUIRE_SECRET_ENCRYPTION`). One run installs one cluster
  (`GENTIAN_ADD_CLUSTER`, `GENTIAN_ACCEPT_CLUSTER`). `bao` is fetched with a
  checksum check.
- Crossplane activates only the provider types the platform composes
  (`crossplane/providers/activation.yaml`; `CROSSPLANE_ACTIVATE_ALL` is the
  way back). Jobs run the pinned `storage-tools` image as non-root; secret
  values reach tools on pipes and files. `--validate` and `--dry-run` change
  nothing, and a teardown holds a lock (`GENTIAN_PURGE_CONFIRM`).
- The installer converges on its pins every run: Argo CD, Image Updater,
  metrics-server, Crossplane packages and the bootstrap Applications, with the
  drift check comparing the rendered chart.
- **A-07** installs metrics-server with kubelet TLS checks on and falls back
  to `--kubelet-insecure-tls` with a warning where the kubelets serve
  certificates without IP SANs; `metricsServer.kubeletInsecureTLS: false`
  stops at once instead. **C-02** waits only for the engines the Composition
  created: MariaDB and the cache exist once a component declares them or
  `systemServices.always` names them.
- **Nodes whose AppArmor stacks container labels** (Ubuntu 26.04 images with
  `kernel.apparmor_restrict_unprivileged_unconfined=1`) cannot deliver
  signals between processes of one container, and PostgreSQL never finishes
  crash recovery. The `node-apparmor` DaemonSet in `kernel-admission` reloads
  containerd's profile with the signal and ptrace rules it lacks before
  kernel-postgres starts, and does nothing elsewhere.

## 8. Edge, DNS, network and mail

- **Envoy Gateway 1.9** with two Gateways in `kernel-edge`, `authenticated`
  and `perimeter`, and the bouncer as ext-authz (AD-13). Zone session cookies
  are stripped before apps see them; a background request gets a 401, not a
  redirect; the authenticated Gateway allows 360 s per request. The
  `gentianos.io/gateway-*` annotations and `routingMode` are gone.
- **Perimeter publishing.** An `expose[].surface: perimeter` entry is published
  only after a perimeter approver enabled it, through a proxy in
  `tenant-<t>-dmz` with its own listener per host; a tenant may publish under
  an alias of the cluster domain.
- **DNS.** `certificates.dnsZones` for tenants on their own domain;
  `certificates.dnsRecords` renders extra records (`<name> <A|CNAME>
  <target>`); external-dns always runs with the DNS provider, behind a tunnel
  too (`certificates.externalDns` is gone).
- **Network policies.** Every component gets `component-<name>` whatever
  delivers it; kernel ingress policies are generated from one inventory; store
  policies are on by default (`STORE_NETWORK_POLICIES`).
- **TURN** as a system service: `requires.services.turn` composes STUNner in
  `system-turn-dmz` with its own load balancer and TURNS on 443; refused on
  tunnel clusters.
- **Mail** runs as `mail.serviceMode: system` (a claim saying `kernel` is
  refused), with a DMZ proxy in `system-mail-dmz`. Apps receive per-app SMTP
  and IMAP records, filled from the external relay in `external` mode and
  empty when there is no mail; `kubectl gentian tenants mail-mode` sets
  `selfhosted`, `external`, `transport-only` or `disabled`. A removed person's
  mailbox is archived or deleted through `MailboxRemoval`. `mailAliases` in
  the operator chart's values forwards addresses of hosted domains to an
  off-cluster address each, written into the Postfix virtual alias map after
  the role aliases; there is no SRS, so a strict SPF check at the target
  fails.

## 9. Data: object store, import, backups and the vault

- **SeaweedFS replaces MinIO** and **Valkey replaces Redis**; every object Job
  uses rclone from `storage-tools`. The object store's volume is
  `systemServices.objectStore.size` (40Gi, never shrunk); Object Lock buckets
  on request.
- **Tenant import:** `kubectl gentian tenants import <file>|--bucket` creates
  and restores through the director; a download comes as one file; `tenants
  retire --purge [--keep-bundles]`; `intoNewTenant` on a restore. The bundle
  (format 5) carries app secrets and authorization stores. Uploads are
  resumable and outlast deadlines, and a refused upload reaches its sender as
  the refusal. A retired tenant's name is reused only with `--reuse-name`.
- **Backups are an add-on's** ([tenant-backup.md](../guides/tenant-backup.md)).
  The platform takes a backup when asked, imports, restores, retires and
  purges; taking backups on a schedule and expiring old ones is the
  `backup-scheduler-ee` add-on's, which the install approves and which
  creates the `TenantExport`s itself. `TenantExportSchedule` and the schedule
  and retention fields of `BackupPolicy` are gone, and a policy write that
  states them is refused with 400; `BackupPolicy` keeps destination,
  encryption and `allowTenantOverride`. `BundleSource.spec.tenant` makes a
  tenant-owned store with its own recipient keys;
  `requires.privileges.apiExtensions` lets an add-on bring CRDs under
  break-glass approval with a digest; tenants' bundles in a shared bucket are
  kept apart as `<bucket>/<tenant>/<export>`. Before an app moves, the two
  newest finished move exports and the last move's own are kept and older
  ones deleted; the backups list carries each export's reason.
- **A run that ends cleans up after itself.** An export or restore that ends,
  Ready or Failed, stops its unit Jobs that still run, discards the Secrets
  they ran with and deletes the key Secret the usher stored for it. The
  usher's restore status names each app with its phase. Two builds of the
  same version that differ past their numbers are refused as not comparable
  (`skipVersionCheck`), not called older.
- **OpenBao on Raft** with daily encrypted snapshots of vault and seal on new
  clusters (`spec.openbao.storage`; existing claims stay on `file` until moved
  by hand, [recovery.md §5](../guides/recovery.md)).

## 10. Observability, audit and supply chain

- One audit envelope from the bouncer, the director and Keycloak, plus Envoy
  access logs ([audit-events.md](../security/audit-events.md)).
  `spec.monitoring` turns on kube-prometheus-stack with Grafana at
  `monitoring.<kernel>` behind the kernel realm; `spec.logForwarding.destination`
  runs Vector to an external or in-cluster destination; alert rules
  `LogForwardingFailing`, `AuditSourceSilent`, `BreakGlassUsed` and
  `RefusalsSpiking`.
- The security pages are complete:
  [principles.md](../security/principles.md), [design.md](../security/design.md),
  [controls.md](../security/controls.md) (K1 to K24) and the audit events.
- **Supply chain.** Dependabot for Go modules, base images and Actions; base
  images and Actions pinned by digest and commit; govulncheck, Trivy, CodeQL,
  SBOM and provenance on every image; images signed keyless in cosign's legacy
  form, which Kyverno 1.13 verifies, with the `gentian-image-signatures`
  policy in Audit for `ghcr.io/gentian-org/*` in tenant namespaces; chart
  schemas checked with kubeconform; nothing is published for a commit that
  failed a check.

## 11. LLM serving

- vLLM instances are delivered (with `gpuAcceleration`), pinned by digest,
  with `memoryLimit` per instance and a hardened pod.
- `spec.llm.personBudget` gives every person a LiteLLM budget and rate limit,
  429 above it; `spec.llm.console.enabled` publishes the LiteLLM console at
  `llm.<kernel>`; `spec.llm.providers` is documented with an external provider
  as the example; `kubectl gentian models list|show|set`. LiteLLM is pinned
  to a version instead of `latest`; `gpuTimeSliceReplicas` is gone.

## 12. Resource plans

The plan catalog lives in git under `clusters/<c>/resource-plans/`, synced by
`gentian-resource-plans` and written by the director; plans carry `units` and
`retired`. Only a cluster administrator moves a tenant between plans
(`PUT /v1/clusters/{c}/tenants/{t}/resources`); `can_set_plan` and
`selfServiceDisabled` are gone. A plan with tenants on it is edited in place:
the plan and each tenant's copy are written in one commit, a change a
tenant's usage does not fit under is refused with 409 naming the tenant and
the resource, and 503 while the operator cannot say who is on the plan.
`PlanOrdering` is the cluster's notice on how to order another plan.

- The documentation is one tree by section with `audience` and `type` front

## 14. APIs

| Kind | What it is |
|---|---|
| `ComponentProfile` | The catalog entry: what a component is and needs |
| `Component` | One installed instance of a profile |
| `ProfileBuild` | One installed build digest of a profile |
| `BundleSource` | A place outside the cluster that holds bundles, the cluster's or a tenant's own |
| `Branding` | The brand every page on the cluster shows |
| `TenantDomain` | A tenant's own domain |
| `MailboxRemoval` | Archiving or deleting a removed person's mailbox |
| `PlanOrdering` | The cluster's notice on how to order another plan |

`TenantExportSchedule`, `TenantBackupPolicy`, and the `app-default`
Composition.

`Cluster` gained `catalogue{storeUrl, sources, extraServices, updates}`,
`certificates.dnsZones`, `certificates.dnsRecords`, `clusterUID`,
`controlPlane{secretsAtRest, evidence}`, `llm.console`, `llm.personBudget`,

`partners`, `platformAccess{roleHolders, accessReview}`,
`platformRoles{admin, breakGlass}`, `systemServices{always, objectStore.size}`
and `status.platform`; it lost `certificates.externalDns`,
`llm.gpuTimeSliceReplicas`, `routingMode` and the default of
`openbao.server`, which the Composition derives as the TLS address.

`Tenant` gained `admin.requireMFA`, `catalogue`, `country`,
`deletion.keepBundles`, `exposures`, `grants`, `groups`, `locales`,
`organization`, `partners`, `partnerCommissioners`,
`platformAccess.adminsEnter`, `privileges`, `security`, `signInFrom`,

`addonGrants` and `placed` on `spec.apps[]`; it lost `domain` and

`BackupPolicy` lost `schedule`, `suspendSchedule`, `retention` and
`status.effectiveSchedule`; `ResourcePlan` gained `units` and `retired` and
lost `selfServiceDisabled`. The director's `backup-schedules` routes are
gone; a restore's status carries `apps[]` with each app's phase.

## 15. Upgrade notes

- **A v0.4 cluster is not upgraded in place.** The namespaces, step numbers,
  kinds, secret paths and addresses are new, and the installer refuses a
  cluster whose Helm releases sit in the old namespaces. The path is a fresh
  install from the release and `kubectl gentian tenants import` of each
  tenant's export bundle; a v0.4 bundle restores into a v0.5 tenant. Keep the
  old cluster's recovery kit until every tenant has arrived.
- **Pin the release in `install.env`:** `GENTIAN_OS_BRANCH=v0.5.0` for the
  platform, `PORTAL_IMAGE_TAG=v0.5.0` for the desktop, the administration
  console and the concierge, whose charts are published under the release
  tag as `0.1.0-v0.5.0` and `0.1.1-v0.5.0`. The default catalog follows the
  ref: the released gentian-apps catalog for a tag, its `develop` catalog
  otherwise.
- **The deployments token needs write access**, and the installer needs
  `git` and `gpg` beside `kubectl helm jq yq openssl curl crossplane python3
  age age-keygen`. Step 0 creates the signing keys; keep `~/.gentian/gnupg`
  with the recovery kit.
- **`install.env` changed.** `GENTIAN_DEPLOYMENTS_REPO` has no default and is
  required. New: `CLUSTER_ADMIN_RECOVERY_EMAIL`, `GENTIAN_KIT_RECIPIENT`,
  `GENTIAN_HANDOVER_WAIT_SECS`, `KERNEL_NETWORK_POLICIES`,
  `STORE_NETWORK_POLICIES`, `GENTIAN_REQUIRE_SECRET_ENCRYPTION`,
  `CROSSPLANE_ACTIVATE_ALL`, `GENTIAN_NO_EXTRA_SERVICES`,
  `GENTIAN_CATALOGUE_URL`, `GENTIAN_STORE_URL`, `GENTIAN_STORE_PARTNER_ISSUER`,
  `GENTIAN_ADD_CLUSTER`, `GENTIAN_ACCEPT_CLUSTER`, `GENTIAN_PURGE_CONFIRM`.
  Removed: `GENTIAN_APPS_REPO`, `GENTIAN_APPS_BRANCH`, `GENTIAN_APPS_AUTH`
  (catalog sources live on the claim), `GENTIAN_OS_IMAGE_REPOSITORY`,
  `GENTIAN_OS_IMAGE_PULL_POLICY`, `PORTAL_SKIP_IMAGE_BUILD`,
  `INFRA_CHART_REPO`, `INFRA_CHART_PRIVATE`. Renamed:
  `GENTIAN_NO_LICENCE_REPORT` is `GENTIAN_NO_USAGE_STATISTICS` and
  `GENTIAN_LICENCE_REPORT_URL` is `GENTIAN_USAGE_STATISTICS_URL`. A
  `GENTIAN_OS_IMAGE_TAG=` line left from an earlier install pins the image;
  remove it. Cluster settings belong on the claim; a value in `install.env`
  overrides it with a warning.
- **`install.sh` flags.** `--prepare-deployment` is step 0;
  `--prepare-tenant` is refused, tenants are created through
  `kubectl gentian tenants create`. New: `--recover`,
  `--rotate-break-glass-key`, `--activate-admin`, `--no-extra-services`,
  `--disable-api-extensions`, `--cluster-infra` with `--purge`, `--check`.
- **The Cluster claim.** `mail.serviceMode: kernel` is `system`.
  `certificates.externalDns`, `llm.gpuTimeSliceReplicas` and `routingMode` are
  refused; leave `openbao.server` unset. Catalog sources, update modes, role holders, partners, monitoring
  and log forwarding are claim fields (§14).
- **Addresses.** The administrator signs in at `https://platform.<domain>/`,
  not `portal.<domain>/login`. Without a DNS provider, publish `<domain>`,
  `*.<domain>`, `*.platform.<domain>` and one `*.<tenant>.<domain>` per
  tenant, plus `turn.`, `monitoring.`, `llm.` and `headlamp.<kernel>` where
  switched on. `corp.<cluster>` is no longer reserved.
- **Secret paths** moved: `gentian-os/kernel/storage/s3` (`s3-admin`),
  `gentian-os/kernel/usage-statistics`, `gentian-os/kernel/repositories/<name>`,
  `gentian-os/tenants/<t>/apps/<app>/{smtp,imap,database,s3,cache,oidc}`. A
  usage-statistics key is not carried over; a rebuilt cluster counts from one.
- **Behavior an operator notices.** `--dry-run` and `--validate` write
  nothing; `--purge` destroys retained data and waits for the Helm uninstalls;
  placed entries cannot be installed or uninstalled by hand; an add-on
  installed as an app is refused; a profile from a tenant's catalog cannot
  claim `trustTier: platform`; Kyverno image-signature findings appear as
  policy reports.
